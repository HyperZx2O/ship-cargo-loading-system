how it flows
OR-tree rejects empties. SW=0 → no pulse, no load.
Adder feeds the register. U7 adds SW+Q, PL latches U6.
BCD makes it readable. Q≥10 → +6, tens shows 1.
Comparator decides. Yellow below, red over, green exact.
Full ship sails itself. QA=B → count+1, MR clears to 00.
