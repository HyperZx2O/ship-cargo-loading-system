THE DOCK

One schematic, four corners.

01

OR-tree validation

Empty ships are turned away before anything latches.

02

Adder plus memory

U7 adds SW and Q. U6 keeps the running total.

03

Readable decimal

Q of 10 or more gets a plus-six correction.

04

A comparator decides

Yellow below, red over, green exact. Green counts and clears.

STEPS

Slow freight, five moves.

1

Set cargo on SW0–SW3. All low means no pulse.

2

Pulse arrival. The sum lands in U6.

3

Read tens and units. Ten and above is corrected.

4

Compare against B0–B3. Watch the three lamps.

5

On exact match, departures rise by one and the dock returns to zero.

PARTS

Eleven chips, no more.

U1 74LS32 · OR-tree + BCD detect
U2 74LS08 · pulse gate + detect
U3 U4 U11 74LS47 · displays
U5 U7 74LS283 · correct + accumulate

U6 74LS193 · dock register
U8 74LS04 · PL invert
U9 74LS85 · comparator
U10 74LS90 · departures
R1 10k pull-up · D1–D3 lamps

DROPPED

What we cut.

Dual-dock routing, the 5-state storm FSM, and the drain engine were removed after Proteus SPICE refused to converge. The comparator outputs already carried the three states that mattered. Full reasoning in report §5.
