THE DOCK

One schematic, four corners.

01

OR-tree validation

Empty ships are turned away before anything latches.

02

Adder plus memory

U7 adds SW and Q. U6 keeps the running total.

03

Readable decimal

Q of 10 or more gets a plus-six correction.

04

A comparator decides

Yellow below, red over, green exact. Green counts and clears.

STEPS

Slow freight, five moves.

1

Set cargo on SW0–SW3. All low means no pulse.

2

Pulse arrival. The sum lands in U6.

3

Read tens and units. Ten and above is corrected.

4

Compare against B0–B3. Watch the three lamps.

5

On exact match, departures rise by one and the dock returns to zero.
